Cookies & Financial Regulations

As a digital banking service operating within the European Union, PiggyBank.com is subject to applicable financial regulations including the Payment Services Directive (PSD2) and General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). Certain cookies related to security, fraud prevention, and transaction integrity are required by these regulations and cannot be disabled.

We process cookie data in accordance with GDPR Article 6(1)(b) — processing necessary for the performance of a contract — and Article 6(1)(f) — legitimate interests including security and fraud prevention.
