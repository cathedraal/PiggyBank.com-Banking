Cookie Duration

Cookies we use fall into two duration categories:

  • Session cookies expire automatically when you close your browser or end your session. They are not stored permanently on your device and are used for temporary functions such as maintaining your login state during a single visit.
  • Persistent cookies remain on your device after your browser is closed, for a defined period or until you manually delete them. We use persistent cookies to remember your preferences and provide a consistent experience on return visits. The duration of persistent cookies varies and is listed in the cookie table above.

We regularly review the duration of cookies we set and aim to keep retention periods to the minimum necessary for their purpose.
