Types of Cookies We Use

These cookies are strictly necessary for PiggyBank.com to operate. They enable core functionality such as security, authentication, and session management. You cannot opt out of these cookies as they are fundamental to our service.

Examples of what essential cookies do:

  • Maintain your login session so you do not have to re-enter your credentials on every page
  • Store your security token to prevent unauthorized access
  • Remember items in your transaction flow
  • Enable secure communication between your device and our servers

Functional cookies allow us to remember choices you make and provide enhanced, more personalized features. They may be set by us or by third-party providers whose services we use.

Examples of what functional cookies do:

  • Remember your preferred currency (EUR, USD, GBP)
  • Store your language and region preferences
  • Remember whether you have dismissed certain notifications or banners
  • Maintain your display preferences across sessions

We use analytics cookies to collect information about how visitors use PiggyBank.com. This helps us understand which features are popular, where users encounter difficulties, and how we can improve our platform. All data collected through analytics cookies is aggregated and anonymized — we cannot identify individual users from this data.

Examples of what analytics cookies do:

  • Count the number of visitors to each page
  • Track how users navigate through the application
  • Measure time spent on different sections
  • Identify technical errors and performance issues

Security cookies are a specialized subset of essential cookies focused specifically on protecting your account and our platform from threats. These cannot be disabled.

Examples of what security cookies do:

  • Detect and prevent fraudulent login attempts
  • Identify suspicious activity patterns
  • Support two-factor authentication flows
  • Enforce rate limiting to prevent automated attacks

These cookies remember choices you have explicitly made to customize your experience on PiggyBank.com.

Examples of what preference cookies do:

  • Remember your chosen theme or display density
  • Store your notification preferences
  • Remember your default card or wallet selection
