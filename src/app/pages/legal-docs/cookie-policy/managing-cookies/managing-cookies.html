Managing & Controlling Cookies

You have several options to control how cookies are used on your device:

Most modern browsers allow you to manage cookie preferences through their settings. You can typically:

  • View cookies currently stored on your device
  • Delete individual cookies or all cookies
  • Block cookies from specific websites
  • Block all third-party cookies
  • Set your browser to notify you when a cookie is being set

Please note that blocking essential cookies will significantly impair the functionality of PiggyBank.com and may prevent you from accessing your account.

Browser-specific instructions:

  • Chrome: Settings → Privacy and Security → Cookies and other site data
  • Firefox: Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data
  • Safari: Preferences → Privacy → Manage Website Data
  • Edge: Settings → Cookies and site permissions

You may opt out of analytics cookies at any time by contacting us at mallindineroman@gmail.com. We will process your request within 14 business days.

Some browsers offer a "Do Not Track" (DNT) setting. PiggyBank.com currently does not respond to DNT signals, as there is no universal standard for how they should be interpreted. We will update this policy if a standard is established.
