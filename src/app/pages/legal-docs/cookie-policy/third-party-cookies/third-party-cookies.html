Third-Party Cookies

Some cookies on PiggyBank.com are set by third-party services that we use to enhance our platform. These third parties have their own privacy and cookie policies, which we encourage you to review.

We use Google Analytics to understand how users interact with our platform. Google Analytics sets cookies that collect information anonymously and report website usage trends without identifying individual visitors. You can opt out of Google Analytics by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on available at tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout.

We work with security service providers to protect our platform from fraud and abuse. These providers may set cookies to help identify and block malicious activity.

We do not allow third-party advertising cookies on PiggyBank.com. We do not sell your data to advertisers or use cookies for targeted advertising purposes. PiggyBank.com is an ad-free platform.
