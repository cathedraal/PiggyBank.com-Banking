Data We Collect

We collect personal data that you provide directly to us, data generated through your use of our services, and in some cases data from third parties.

When you register, use, or contact us through PiggyBank.com, you may provide:

  • Identity data — first name, last name, date of birth
  • Contact data — email address, phone number, postal address
  • Financial data — card number, card type, expiry date, CVC, account balance, transaction history
  • Authentication data — login credentials, security questions
  • Communication data — messages you send us through the support popup, emails, or other channels
  • Preference data — your selected currency, language, notification settings

When you use PiggyBank.com, we automatically collect:

  • Technical data — IP address, browser type and version, operating system, device type, screen resolution
  • Usage data — pages visited, features used, time spent, clicks, navigation paths
  • Transaction data — amount, currency, date, time, and type of transactions you perform
  • Session data — login timestamps, session duration, logout events
  • Security — failed login attempts, suspicious activity flags, device fingerprint

In some cases we may receive data about you from third parties, including:

  • Identity verification providers for fraud prevention purposes
  • Payment network operators such as Visa and Mastercard
  • Analytics providers such as Google Analytics
  • Public registers where required by financial regulation
