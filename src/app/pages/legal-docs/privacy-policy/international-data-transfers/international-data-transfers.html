International Data Transfers

PiggyBank.com primarily stores and processes your data within the European Economic Area (EEA). Where data is transferred outside the EEA, we ensure appropriate safeguards are in place, including:

  • Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) approved by the European Commission
  • Adequacy decisions recognizing the destination country's data protection standards
  • Binding Corporate Rules where applicable

You may request details of the safeguards we use for international transfers by contacting mallindineroman@gmail.com.
