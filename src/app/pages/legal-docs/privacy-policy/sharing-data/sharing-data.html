6

Sharing Your Data

We do not sell your personal data to third parties. We may share your data in the following limited circumstances:

We work with carefully selected third-party service providers who process data on our behalf. These include:

  • Cloud infrastructure providers for data hosting
  • Analytics providers for platform improvement
  • Security and fraud prevention services
  • Email delivery services for notifications
  • Identity verification providers

All service providers are bound by data processing agreements and are required to process your data only as instructed by us.

To process card transactions, we share necessary transaction data with payment networks such as Visa and Mastercard, and where applicable, correspondent banking partners.

We may disclose your data to regulatory authorities, law enforcement agencies, or courts where required by law, including but not limited to:

  • Bundesanstalt für Finanzdienstleistungsaufsicht (BaFin)
  • Financial intelligence units under AML obligations
  • Courts and tribunals in legal proceedings

In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of all or part of our business, your personal data may be transferred to the acquiring entity. We will notify you of any such change and ensure your data remains protected.

We may share your data with other parties where you have provided explicit consent to do so.
